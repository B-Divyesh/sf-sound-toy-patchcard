Privacy

How Patchcard handles your data

Effective September 5, 2026

The short version

Patchcard stores saved cards in your browser. The site has no accounts, analytics, advertising cookies, or hosted card database.

Cards and demo data

Real cards use one browser storage area. The demo uses a separate sample area and deletes it when you start for real.

Audio and exports

Audio previews and WAV files are made on your device. Patchcard does not upload them.

Share links

A share link contains the card name, toy details, controls, and waveform data. It leaves out WAV audio by default.

Anyone with the link can read those settings. Do not put personal information in card names or descriptions.

Network requests

The site loads no third-party scripts, fonts, analytics, or storage services. The hosting provider may keep ordinary access logs.

Your privacy choices

You can delete cards in the app or clear this site’s browser data. We cannot retrieve or delete data we never receive.
